# SOP: Editing & Reviewing Content

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-18
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: Writer submits a draft article for review.
- **Inputs**: Draft content, target keyword list, writing guidelines.
- **Outputs**: Approved draft ready for publishing.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to edit and review content drafts. It ensures drafts contain target keywords, follow our style rules (no contractions, no em dashes), and provide actual value. This maintains high quality standards.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Docs
- Grammar check tool (Grammarly)
- Readability tool (Hemingway App)

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Check Keyword Presence**
   - Action: Search the draft document for the target primary and secondary keywords.
   - Expected Result: The primary keyword is used naturally in the title, first paragraph, and one subheading.
2. **Scan for Contractions**
   - Action: Search the document for common contractions (like *do not*, *cannot*, *will not*). Replace them with complete words (e.g. use "do not", "cannot", "will not").
   - Expected Result: The draft contains zero contractions.
3. **Scan for Em Dashes**
   - Action: Search the document for em dashes (, ). Replace them with commas, colons, or simple sentence breaks.
   - Expected Result: The draft contains zero em dashes.
4. **Remove AI Fluff and Jargon**
   - Action: Locate and delete vague, generic sentences or robotic phrases (e.g. delete "In today's fast-paced digital world").
   - Expected Result: The text sounds like a real person wrote it.
5. **Verify Readability Score**
   - Action: Copy the text into the Hemingway App. Check that the readability score is at or below an eighth-grade level.
   - Expected Result: The text uses simple, clear phrasing.
6. **Review Call to Action**
   - Action: Ensure the article ends with a clear call to action directing readers to contact Digital Neighbour or call the client phone number.
   - Expected Result: A direct lead generation element is present.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Primary keyword present in title and first paragraph.
- [ ] Zero contractions present in the draft.
- [ ] Zero em dashes present in the draft.
- [ ] Readability score is at or below eighth-grade level.
- [ ] Strong CTA included at the end.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Draft has too many contractions | Writer used their default writing habits | Use the Find and Replace tool in Google Docs to locate and replace common contractions automatically. |
| Text reads like robotic AI content | Writer generated the article without editing | Rewrite key paragraphs. Add specific client details, local references, and real statistics to make the content authentic. |
