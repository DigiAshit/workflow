# SOP: Content Structure for AI and SEO Visibility

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-17
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior Content Strategist
- **Trigger**: New blog post or landing page content creation is scheduled.
- **Inputs**: Mapped keywords, target questions, client business context.
- **Outputs**: Structured content brief optimized for search engine crawlers and AI answer engines.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to structure website content to maximize visibility in search results and AI answer engines (like ChatGPT and Gemini). It details how to format headings, integrate semantic terms, and satisfy search intent.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Docs (client project folder)
- Keyword research sheet
- Google Search Console (for PAA questions)

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Analyze Search Intent**
   - Action: Search for the primary keyword on Google. Identify whether the search results show guides, service pages, or list articles.
   - Expected Result: You confirm what type of content searchers expect.
2. **Design Heading Hierarchy**
   - Action: Create a heading outline. Use exactly one H1 heading at the top, followed by H2 and H3 subheadings to structure the topics.
   - Expected Result: The outline has a logical hierarchy.
3. **Integrate Primary Keyword**
   - Action: Plan to place the primary keyword in the H1 tag, the first paragraph, and one H2 subheading.
   - Expected Result: Keyword placement is mapped.
4. **Integrate Semantic Keywords**
   - Action: Identify relevant terms related to the main topic. Plan to use these terms naturally in the body paragraphs.
   - Expected Result: Semantic terms are integrated.
5. **Add PAA Questions**
   - Action: Find two relevant questions from the Google People Also Ask box. Plan to answer these questions directly in the text.
   - Expected Result: Core user questions are included.
6. **Include E-E-A-T Elements**
   - Action: Plan to include industry statistics, quotes from the business founders (Sagar or Pranav), and clear credentials.
   - Expected Result: Authority signals are built into the brief.
7. **Use Bullet Points and Tables**
   - Action: Identify sections where data can be presented in comparison tables or simple lists.
   - Expected Result: The structure is optimized for Google rich snippets and AI overview extraction.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Structure has exactly one H1 tag.
- [ ] Heading hierarchy uses H2 and H3 tags correctly.
- [ ] Primary keyword placed in first paragraph.
- [ ] PAA questions integrated and answered.
- [ ] Content outline includes bullet lists or tables.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Content lacks authority signals | Writer did not use real data or company info | Include a specific quote from Sagar or Pranav, and reference actual business numbers or case studies. |
| Too many keywords are used | Keyword stuffing | Remove extra keyword references. Focus on writing high-quality, helpful paragraphs that flow naturally. |
