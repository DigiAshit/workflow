# SOP: Monthly Website Review & Performance Audit

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-26
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: The monthly reporting cycle is complete.
- **Inputs**: Google Search Console data, GA4 data, active keywords list, competitor rankings.
- **Outputs**: Performance analysis notes, priorities list for the next month.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to conduct the monthly website review. We analyze organic traffic, conversion trends, and search ranking shifts. This helps us identify performance issues and plan work for the next month.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics 4
- Ahrefs (or keyword tracker)
- ClickUp

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Analyze Search Console Trends**
   - Action: Open GSC and compare performance data from the last thirty days to the previous period. Check for click or impression drops.
   - Expected Result: You identify search traffic trends.
2. **Analyze Traffic and Conversions**
   - Action: Open GA4, navigate to reports, and review traffic sources. Verify the number of conversion events (form submissions and phone clicks).
   - Expected Result: You measure lead changes.
3. **Check Keyword Rankings**
   - Action: Open the keyword tracker and review the positions of primary target terms. Log any ranking drops.
   - Expected Result: You identify ranking shifts.
4. **Identify Crawl and Indexing Errors**
   - Action: Open GSC, go to the indexing page, and check if any active pages have indexing errors or crawl warnings.
   - Expected Result: Technical issues are identified.
5. **Review Competitor Activity**
   - Action: Search for primary keywords on Google. Check if competitors have updated their meta titles or published new content targeting the terms.
   - Expected Result: Competitor shifts are noted.
6. **Plan Next Month Priorities**
   - Action: Create a list of the top three SEO tasks for the next month based on the review.
   - Expected Result: Next month goals are defined.
7. **Create Monthly Task in ClickUp**
   - Action: Log the review notes and next month goals in the client monthly tracking card in ClickUp.
   - Expected Result: Work plans are updated.

### SEO Review

- [ ] Page speeds checked and reviewed.
- [ ] Schema validation checked.
### Business Review
- [ ] Total business leads reviewed and checked.
- [ ] Goal conversion rates reviewed and checked.
- [ ] Competitor rankings and updates review completed.
### Action Plan
- [ ] Priorities for the next month defined.
- [ ] Actionable recommendations documented for the client.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Core keyword ranks dropped significantly | Google rolled out an algorithm update | Review search results to see what pages are ranking now. Adjust content quality, EEAT signals, and internal links. |
| GA4 conversion tracking shows zero leads but client reports inquiries | GTM tracking tags were broken during website update | Open GTM preview mode, submit a test form, check why tags did not fire, and repair the GTM triggers. |
