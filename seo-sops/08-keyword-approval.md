# SOP: Keyword Approval Process

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-08
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Owner Role**: Client Relationship Manager
- **Trigger**: Keyword research sheet is completed.
- **Inputs**: Completed keyword research sheet.
- **Outputs**: Documented internal and client keyword approvals.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to get keywords approved internally by Sagar and externally by the client. Documented approval ensures we target search terms that support client business goals before we build content.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Gmail
- ClickUp
- Google Sheets (client project folder)

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Review Keywords Internally**
   - Action: Share the keyword research sheet link with Sagar on ClickUp and ask him to review the keyword mapping.
   - Expected Result: Sagar reviews and provides comments or gives approval.
2. **Apply Internal Revisions**
   - Action: Adjust the target keywords or URL mappings if Sagar requests changes.
   - Expected Result: The keyword sheet is updated based on internal feedback.
3. **Prepare Client Approval Email**
   - Action: Open Gmail and draft a keyword approval email. Explain why we selected the primary search terms. Include the Google Sheet link.
   - Expected Result: The draft email is prepared and clear.
4. **Send Client Email**
   - Action: Send the email to the client, asking them to review and approve the target keywords.
   - Expected Result: The email is sent.
5. **Document Client Revisions**
   - Action: If the client requests changes, update the keyword research sheet to reflect their input.
   - Expected Result: Adjustments are logged.
6. **Log Final Approval**
   - Action: Once the client approves the final keyword list via email, copy the email text and paste it into the ClickUp task.
   - Expected Result: Client approval is officially documented.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Keyword sheet completed and filled.
- [ ] All website URLs mapped to keywords.
- [ ] Search intent verified for all selected terms.
- [ ] Priority keywords highlighted.
- [ ] Sagar approval received and documented.
- [ ] Client approval received and documented.
- [ ] Changes and feedback implemented.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Client rejects key industry terms | Client does not understand search volume or SEO value | Explain the search volume, intent, and competitor rankings for the target terms. Send a simple summary. |
| Client takes a long time to approve | Client is busy running their business | Send a reminder email after five business days, or call them to get verbal approval. |
