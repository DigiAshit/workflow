- **Version**: 2.0

- **Inputs**: Approved keywords list, Ahrefs tool, offpage planning spreadsheet.
- **Outputs**: Completed Offpage Plan sheet with target URLs and anchor text targets.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to create the off-page link building plan. We audit competitor backlinks to design an anchor text strategy. This builds domain authority and improves search rankings safely.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Ahrefs
- Google Sheets (client project folder)

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Analyze Competitor Backlinks**
   - Action: Enter the target page URL in Ahrefs and analyze the backlink profile of top competing pages. Conduct a complete competitor backlink audit.
   - Expected Result: You determine the average number of backlinks needed to rank.
2. **Conduct Link Gap Analysis**
   - Action: Compare the client backlink profile with competitors. Identify high-quality websites that link to competitors but not the client.
   - Expected Result: Link gap opportunities are documented.
3. **Define Anchor Text Strategy**
   - Action: Plan anchor text distribution. Set seventy percent of anchors to branded terms, twenty percent to partial match keywords, and ten percent to exact match keywords.
   - Expected Result: Safe anchor distribution is planned.
4. **Set Goals and Targets**
   - Action: Assign monthly goals and link acquisition targets to each mapped URL in the spreadsheet.
   - Expected Result: Monthly targets are scheduled.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Target keywords are too competitive for the link budget | Competitors have thousands of links | Focus the link building efforts on long-tail subpages first to build topic authority before targeting main terms. |
| Anchor text ratios look unnatural | Too many exact keyword matches planned | Shift the plan to include more branded anchors or general click text (e.g. "click here", "website link") to avoid penalties. |
