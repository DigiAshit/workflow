# SOP: Local Offpage Plan

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-21
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Owner Role**: SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: Keywords strategy is approved and onboarding is complete.
- **Inputs**: Client business NAP details (Name, Address, Phone), competitor citations list, local citations template.
- **Outputs**: Completed Local Offpage Plan sheet, list of citation opportunities.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to build a local off-page SEO plan. We audit and plan citations on directory websites to build local search relevance and verify the business NAP consistency. This helps our clients rank in the Google Maps Map Pack.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Sheets (client project folder)
- Local citation checking tool (e.g. WhiteSpark)
- Google Search

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Audit Existing Citations**
   - Action: Search for the client phone number and brand name in Google. Log all existing directory listings in your sheet.
   - Expected Result: You locate existing profile listings.
2. **Verify NAP Consistency**
   - Action: Check that the Business Name, Street Address, and Phone Number match exactly on every directory profile. Mark any discrepancies.
   - Expected Result: NAP errors are logged for correction.
3. **Research Competitor Citations**
   - Action: Search for top competitor phone numbers and identify where they have directory listings. Find listings of the top three competitors.
   - Expected Result: You find new citation opportunities.
4. **Select Target Directory Sites**
   - Action: List high-authority local directory sites that the client is missing.
   - Expected Result: A target citation checklist is built.
5. **Plan NAP Next Step**
   - Action: If NAP discrepancies are found during the audit, define a clear next step action in the plan to clean up or correct the citation details.
   - Expected Result: NAP cleanup steps are planned.
6. **Set Monthly Targets and GMB Actions**
   - Action: Assign monthly citation build targets and list required monthly Google My Business activities (e.g. posting monthly updates, adding photos).
   - Expected Result: The target schedule is set.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Top local citations identified based on the top three competitors.
- [ ] Existing citation profiles audited.
- [ ] NAP consistency reviewed and the next step defined to fix errors.
- [ ] Monthly citation targets defined.
- [ ] Monthly Google My Business activities included in the plan.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Old business address is live on directories | Business relocated but directories were not updated | Log into the incorrect profiles and update the address. If access is lost, contact directory support. |
| Duplicate listings exist on the same directory | Multiple accounts were created | Contact the directory webmaster and request to merge or delete the duplicate profile. |
