# SOP: Press Release Copywriting & Formatting

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-32
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: Client requires PR content for brand visibility.
- **Inputs**: Client briefing document, target keywords, location information, word count limits.
- **Outputs**: Formatted Press Release document ready for distribution.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to write and format Press Releases. It ensures that media releases follow standard layout structures, contain engaging hooks, and present client brand details correctly. This helps Digital Neighbour gain media coverage for Kiwi client projects.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Drive and Google Docs
- Client briefing document

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Identify Campaign Details**
   - Action: Open the client briefing sheet to locate the target keywords, target location, and word count requirements.
   - Expected Result: You note the target parameters for the media release.
2. **Add Media Release Header**
   - Action: Type "MEDIA RELEASE" in large, bold capital letters at the very top of the Google Document.
   - Expected Result: The header is set correctly.
3. **Draft compelling Headline**
   - Action: Write a headline that contains a hook and a primary keyword.
   - Expected Result: The headline is engaging and clear.
4. **Draft Lead Paragraph**
   - Action: Write a short lead paragraph that details the Who, What, When, Where, and Why of the news.
   - Expected Result: The lead paragraph contains all core facts.
5. **Add Director Quote**
   - Action: Write a paragraph containing a quote from a client director detailing the project vision.
   - Expected Result: A professional quote is included in the document.
6. **Add Corporate Boilerplate**
   - Action: Write a short section at the bottom titled "About [Client Brand Name]" detailing the history and mission of the business.
   - Expected Result: The corporate boilerplate is set.
7. **Verify Word Count**
   - Action: Select all text and check the word count settings in Google Docs.
   - Expected Result: The total word count is between five hundred and seven hundred words.
8. **Check Spelling and Grammar**
   - Action: Run a spelling and grammar check on the draft.
   - Expected Result: The text contains zero spelling errors.
9. **Send for Approval**
   - Action: Share the Google Document link with Sagar to get final approval.
   - Expected Result: Sagar receives the link for review.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] MEDIA RELEASE header placed at the top.
- [ ] Headline contains a hook and keyword.
- [ ] Lead paragraph answers Who, What, When, Where, and Why.
- [ ] Quote from client director included.
- [ ] Corporate boilerplate included at the bottom.
- [ ] Total word count is between five hundred and seven hundred words.
- [ ] Sent to Sagar for approval.

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The news editor rejects the Press Release | The headline lacks a news angle | Rewrite the headline to focus on local impact or innovation instead of advertising features. |
| The text is too long or too short | The content was not edited | Delete unnecessary paragraphs or add more background details to match the required length. |
