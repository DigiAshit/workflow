# SOP: AI Website Structure

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-27
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior Content Strategist
- **Trigger**: Strategy requires structuring new website sections or pages.
- **Inputs**: Target keywords, client business details, formatting outline guidelines.
- **Outputs**: Properly structured webpage content optimized for search and AI engine visibility.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to structure website content for search engines and AI engines. It ensures target keywords are placed correctly, formatting is consistent, and content is clean and high-quality.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Docs (client project folder)
- Keyword research sheet
- Readability tool (Hemingway App)

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Insert Primary and Secondary Keywords**
   - Action: Place the primary keyword in the main heading and first paragraph. Place secondary keywords naturally in the body text.
   - Expected Result: Key terms are distributed correctly.
2. **Plan Internal Links**
   - Action: Add two to three links to other service pages or articles on the client website.
   - Expected Result: Internal linking paths are built.
3. **Review Schema Opportunities**
   - Action: Identify content sections (like FAQs or business address) that qualify for schema markup and add the schema tags.
   - Expected Result: Structured data opportunities are mapped.
4. **Check Grammar and Spelling**
   - Action: Run a grammar checker on the drafted content and correct any spelling mistakes.
   - Expected Result: Text contains zero grammar errors.
5. **Remove AI Fluff and Jargon**
   - Action: Delete robotic intro sentences, fluff words, and corporate jargon. Ensure the writing style matches the target tone.
   - Expected Result: The writing style is natural and active.
6. **Check for Headings Duplication**
   - Action: Scan the document to ensure no two headings are identical.
   - Expected Result: All headings are unique.
7. **Optimize Heading Format**
   - Action: Check that headings use a hierarchical order (H1 to H2 to H3). Format the text structure using clear paragraphs and bulleted pointers.
   - Expected Result: Text readability is optimized for mobile and desktop screens.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
### SEO Setup
- [ ] Primary keyword present in title and first paragraph.
- [ ] Secondary keywords present in body paragraphs.
- [ ] Internal links added with natural anchor text.
- [ ] Schema opportunities reviewed and added.
### Quality & Tone
- [ ] Grammar and spelling checked.
- [ ] Generic AI fluff and jargon words removed.
- [ ] Brand voice and tone match guidelines.
- [ ] No duplicate headings or duplicate content blocks exist.
- [ ] Unique industry insights or quotes included.
- [ ] Clear call to action (CTA) button text included.
### UX & Formatting
- [ ] Heading formatting is correct (Heading -> Subheading -> Pointers/Paragraph).

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Writing contains robotic structures | Content was drafted using default AI prompts | Rephrase paragraphs to use short sentences. Use active voice and write in simple plain English. |
| Formatting looks messy on mobile | Pointers are too long | Keep bullet points concise and limit them to one or two lines max. |
