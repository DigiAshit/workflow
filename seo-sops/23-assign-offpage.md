# SOP: Handover & Task Assignment to Off-page Team

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-23
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: Off-page plan and local off-page plan sheets are approved.
- **Inputs**: Approved offpage plans, off-page team members list.
- **Outputs**: Tasks assigned to the off-page team in ClickUp.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to assign link building and local citation tasks to our off-page execution team. A clear handover ensures that the team has the target URLs, keywords, and anchor rules required to build links.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- ClickUp
- Offpage Plan spreadsheets

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Locate Off-page Plan Links**
   - Action: Copy the sharing links for the completed Local Offpage Plan and general Offpage Plan.
   - Expected Result: You have the sheet links.
2. **Create Assignment Task in ClickUp**
   - Action: Go to the client project space in ClickUp, create a new task named Execute Monthly Offpage Plan, and assign it to the off-page team.
   - Expected Result: You create the task.
3. **Insert Plan Details**
   - Action: Paste the spreadsheet links in the task description. Write clear instructions specifying the target landing page URLs, target keywords, and anchor rules.
   - Expected Result: Task description contains all required details.
4. **Set Deadlines**
   - Action: Set the task start date to the first day of the month and the due date to the final day of the month.
   - Expected Result: Task dates are set.
5. **Confirm Handoff**
   - Action: Add a comment tagging the off-page team leader, asking them to confirm receipt of the monthly plan.
   - Expected Result: The team leader is notified.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Task assigned to the correct off-page team member.
- [ ] Monthly execution task contains both plan sheet links.
- [ ] Landing page URLs and anchor instructions are clear.
- [ ] Start and due dates set correctly.
- [ ] Team leader confirmation received.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Off-page team cannot open sheet links | Google Sheets permissions are private | Change the Google Sheets share settings to "Anyone inside Digital Neighbour with the link can edit". |
| Link building starts late in the month | Handoff task was created late | Prepare the offpage plan during the final week of the previous month so assignment happens on day one. |
