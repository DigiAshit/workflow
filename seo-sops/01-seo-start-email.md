# SOP: SEO Start Official Email to Client

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-01
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Owner Role**: Client Relationship Manager
- **Trigger**: Client signs the contract for Search Engine Optimisation services.
- **Inputs**: Signed client contract, client contact email address, client business name.
- **Outputs**: Official welcome email sent, client folder created in Google Drive.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to send the official welcome email to a new client. It ensures we gather the correct login credentials and set clear expectations for the campaign. This helps Digital Neighbour build trust with Kiwi business owners from day one.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Gmail (Digital Neighbour account)
- ClickUp
- Google Drive (agency shared drive)

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Create Client Folder**
   - Action: Create a new folder for the client in the Digital Neighbour Google Drive under the SEO folder.
   - Expected Result: A new folder with the client business name is visible in Google Drive.
2. **Open Email Template**
   - Action: Open Gmail, click Compose, and load the SEO Welcome Email Template.
   - Expected Result: A blank draft with the standard welcome layout appears.
3. **Insert Client Details**
   - Action: Replace the placeholder values in the template with the client name, business name, and target website URL.
   - Expected Result: The draft contains the correct client details.
4. **Request Key Access**
   - Action: List the required login details in the email, including Google My Business access, website editor login details, and Google Search Console access.
   - Expected Result: The email clearly asks for the correct credentials.
5. **Share Project Timeline**
   - Action: Outline the first month plan, starting with the website audit and keyword research phases.
   - Expected Result: The client knows what tasks we will perform in the first four weeks.
6. **Send the Email**
   - Action: Double check the email address, subject line, and content, then click the Send button.
   - Expected Result: The email is sent and a copy is visible in the Sent folder.
7. **Update ClickUp Task**
   - Action: Mark the task named SEO Start Official Email to Client as complete in ClickUp and paste the email sent date.
   - Expected Result: The task status updates to complete in ClickUp.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Welcome email sent within twenty-four hours of signing the contract.
- [ ] Welcome email requests GMB, website, and GSC logins.
- [ ] Draft contains zero typos or placeholder text.
- [ ] ClickUp task is marked as complete.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Client does not reply to the access request | Email went to spam folder or client is busy | Wait three business days. Send a friendly follow up email or make a quick phone call. |
| Client does not know how to share GMB access | Client is not tech-savvy | Send the standard Digital Neighbour PDF guide that shows how to share GMB access. |
