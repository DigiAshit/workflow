## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-09
- **Version**: 2.1
- **Owner Role**: Senior SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: Client keyword list is approved.
- **Inputs**: Approved keyword list, client content sheet, Unified AI Blog Workflow, competitor SERP examples, target internal pages.
- **Outputs**: Completed content plan sheet, blog articles created as per the workflow, visual and internal-link plan, Sagar approval.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to create the content plan and write blog content for the website. It ensures that target keywords are mapped, competitor structure is reviewed, internal links are planned, visuals are selected for usefulness, and content is generated following the strict modular, E-E-A-T focused writing guidelines.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Sheets (client project folder)
- Keyword research sheet
- Unified AI Blog Workflow (`workflow.md`)
- Google Docs
- Competitor SERP examples

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Open Content Plan Template**
   - Action: Open the client Google Drive folder, go to the `03_Onpage_and_Content` sub-folder, and open the Content Plan sheet.
   - Expected Result: The plan template loads.
2. **Assign Target Keywords**
   - Action: Copy the approved keywords and map them to specific planned page titles or blog topics in column A.
   - Expected Result: Keywords are assigned.
3. **Review Ranking Content Patterns**
   - Action: Open three to five ranking pages for the primary keyword. Record the page type, heading structure, visual types, table or list usage, CTA placement, author signals, date handling, and internal-link patterns.
   - Expected Result: The content plan includes a short competitor pattern note.
4. **Plan Internal Links**
   - Action: Specify which main service pages, category pages, tool pages, and supporting blog posts the new article must link to. Use natural anchors that match the reader's next step.
   - Expected Result: Internal linking paths are defined before drafting.
5. **Plan Visuals and Rich Elements**
   - Action: Decide where screenshots, annotated examples, diagrams, charts, tables, lists, FAQs, or callouts are needed. Each visual must explain, prove, or demonstrate a point.
   - Expected Result: The draft has a clear visual and rich-element plan.
6. **Initialize Blog Creation**
   - Action: Select the target topic and review the client details in the workflow file.
   - Expected Result: You confirm the client website, target email, and folder.
7. **Draft Content as per Workflow**
   - Action: Write the content following the rules in `workflow.md` and `seo-sops/17-content-structure-ai-seo.md`. Ensure you use short modular paragraphs, no contractions, and no em dashes.
   - Expected Result: The article draft is written.
8. **Integrate E-E-A-T, Q&A, and Freshness**
   - Action: Insert real data, screenshots from trusted tools, quotes from the founders (Sagar or Pranav), author or reviewer credentials, publish or updated date guidance, and direct answers to two to five target questions.
   - Expected Result: Content authority is established.
9. **Submit for Sagar Approval**
   - Action: Send the draft link to Sagar on ClickUp for review and approval.
   - Expected Result: Sagar reviews and approves the draft.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Content contains contractions | Writer used default writing habits | Use the search tool to locate and replace contractions with complete words before submitting. |
| Links are broken in the plan | Page URLs changed | Verify the target service page URLs on the live website and update the plan. |
| Content plan copies competitors too closely | Competitor review was treated as a template | Use competitor patterns only to understand reader expectations. Keep examples, structure, and wording original to the client. |
| Draft lacks useful visuals | Visual planning was skipped | Add screenshots, annotated examples, diagrams, charts, or tables only where they help explain or prove the section. |
