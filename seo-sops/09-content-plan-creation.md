# SOP: Content Plan & Creation

## 1. Document Control
- **ID**: dn-seo-sop-09
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Owner Role**: Senior SEO Specialist
- **Trigger**: Client keyword list is approved.
- **Inputs**: Approved keyword list, client content sheet, Unified AI Blog Workflow.
- **Outputs**: Completed content plan sheet, blog articles created as per the workflow, Sagar approval.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
This Standard Operating Procedure explains how to create the content plan and write blog content for the website. It ensures that target keywords are mapped, internal links are planned, and content is generated following the strict modular, E-E-A-T focused writing guidelines.

---

## 3. Required Tools
- Google Sheets (client project folder)
- Keyword research sheet
- Unified AI Blog Workflow (`workflow.md`)
- Google Docs

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Open Content Plan Template**
   - Action: Open the client Google Drive folder, go to the `03_Onpage_and_Content` sub-folder, and open the Content Plan sheet.
   - Expected Result: The plan template loads.
2. **Assign Target Keywords**
   - Action: Copy the approved keywords and map them to specific planned page titles or blog topics in column A.
   - Expected Result: Keywords are assigned.
3. **Plan Internal Links**
   - Action: Specify which main service pages or category pages the new articles must link to.
   - Expected Result: Internal linking paths are defined.
4. **Initialize Blog Creation**
   - Action: Select the target topic and review the client details in the workflow file.
   - Expected Result: You confirm the client website, target email, and folder.
5. **Draft Content as per Workflow**
   - Action: Write the content following the rules in `workflow.md`. Ensure you use short modular paragraphs (two to four sentences), no contractions, and no em dashes.
   - Expected Result: The article draft is written.
6. **Integrate E-E-A-T and Q&A**
   - Action: Insert real data, quotes from the founders (Sagar or Pranav), and answer two target questions naturally in the body text.
   - Expected Result: Content authority is established.
7. **Submit for Sagar Approval**
   - Action: Send the draft link to Sagar on ClickUp for review and approval.
   - Expected Result: Sagar reviews and approves the draft.

---

## 5. Quality Control & Measurable Checks
- [ ] Target keywords assigned to all planned pages.
- [ ] Internal links planned and documented.
- [ ] Content created as per the workflow (modular paragraphs, no contractions, no em dashes).
- [ ] Content approved by Sagar.

---

## 6. Common Problems & Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Content contains contractions | Writer used default writing habits | Use the search tool to locate and replace contractions with complete words before submitting. |
| Links are broken in the plan | Page URLs changed | Verify the target service page URLs on the live website and update the plan. |
