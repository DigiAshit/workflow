# Claude Prompt: Internal Linking Opportunity Finder

You are an expert SEO strategist specialising in internal linking, topical authority and organic search growth.

I will provide you with:

1. **Target URL:** The page I want to improve
2. **Target keywords:** Keywords where this page is currently ranking between positions 8–20 in Google Search Console
3. **Website URLs/content:** A list of relevant pages from the website, including their URLs and page titles. Where available, I may also provide the page content.

Your task is to identify **high-value internal linking opportunities** that could strengthen the target page's relevance and authority for the target keywords.

## Target Page

URL:

[INSERT TARGET URL]

## Target Keywords

[INSERT KEYWORDS]

## Current GSC Data

[INSERT KEYWORD + CLICKS + IMPRESSIONS + POSITION DATA]

## Website Pages

[PASTE URL LIST, SITEMAP OR CRAWL DATA HERE]

## Your Analysis

For each target keyword:

1. Identify the most relevant pages that could link to the target page.
2. Prioritise pages that are topically relevant and already have strong contextual relevance.
3. Do not recommend links simply because a keyword appears on the page.
4. Look at the overall topic and search intent of each page.
5. Identify the most natural location within the existing content where an internal link could be added.
6. Recommend suitable anchor text.
7. Avoid excessive exact-match anchor text.
8. Use natural variations and partial-match anchors where appropriate.
9. Do not recommend links from irrelevant pages.
10. Do not recommend creating new content unless absolutely necessary.
11. Prioritise existing pages that already receive organic traffic or have strong topical relevance.
12. Avoid recommending multiple links from pages where the link would feel forced or unnatural.

## Output

Create a table with:

| Priority | Source URL | Target URL | Suggested Anchor Text | Suggested Placement | Target Keyword | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then provide:

### Top 5 Internal Linking Opportunities

Identify the five strongest opportunities and explain briefly why each one should be prioritised.

### Anchor Text Strategy

Provide 5–10 natural anchor text variations that could be used when linking to the target page.

Do not overuse the exact target keyword.

### Implementation Plan

Give me a simple implementation plan showing:

- Which links should be added first
- Which links are optional
- Which links should not be added
- Any existing internal links that should potentially be updated rather than adding new ones

### Important SEO Rules

Do not recommend keyword stuffing.

Do not recommend adding links where they are not contextually useful.

Do not assume that more internal links are always better.

Focus on **relevance, context, authority and user experience**.

The objective is to strengthen the target page's internal topical signals using the website's existing content, not to artificially manipulate rankings.

If the information provided is insufficient to make a confident recommendation, clearly state what additional data is required rather than guessing.
